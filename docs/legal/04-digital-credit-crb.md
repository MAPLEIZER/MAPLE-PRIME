# Chapter 4 — Digital credit providers and CRBs

The CBK Digital Credit Providers Regulations govern licensed DCPs and include licensing, conduct, customer-information, credit-information and consumer-protection requirements. They are especially relevant to KDR's lender/app/sender mapping work.

The Banking (Credit Reference Bureau) Regulations govern CRBs and the exchange/dispute framework for credit information.

## Four facts KDR never collapses

1. CBK DCP licensing status.
2. ODPC controller/processor registration status.
3. CRB subscriber/approved-provider regulatory status.
4. Evidence that a particular provider actually submitted **this person's** credit information.

These are related but distinct facts.

## Message triage examples

Loan marketing, approval/disbursement, repayment reminders, collections and CRB notices can be classified separately. Classification is an evidence-organizing tool; it is not a legal conclusion.

Authoritative sources: Kenya Law Legal Notice 46 of 2022 and Legal Notice 55 of 2020; CBK official directories.
