# Chapter 2 — Data Protection Act and General Regulations

The Data Protection Act is KDR's central legal reference for personal-data processing. Important teaching areas include registration, processing principles, data-subject rights, transparency, consent, lawful purpose, security, rectification/erasure, complaints and remedies.

## Practical map

- **Sections 18–21:** registration of controllers/processors and the register.
- **Section 25:** data-protection principles.
- **Section 26:** core data-subject rights.
- **Sections 29–32:** notice, lawful processing and consent-related duties.
- **Section 40:** rectification and erasure.
- **Sections 56–66:** complaints, enforcement, penalties and compensation framework.

The General Regulations operationalize many of these requirements, including consent information, rights procedures, retention/security and controller-processor arrangements.

## Message-related examples

A loan message may justify checking whether a provider had the right phone number, the purpose for using it, whether marketing consent/opt-out rules were followed, whether third-party data was disclosed, or whether inaccurate data should be corrected. KDR should present these as **questions and possible provisions**, not automatic findings.

Authoritative sources: Kenya Law, Data Protection Act (Cap. 411C) and Data Protection (General) Regulations.
