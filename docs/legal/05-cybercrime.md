# Chapter 5 — Computer Misuse and Cybercrimes

The Computer Misuse and Cybercrimes Act (Cap. 79C) covers offences and investigative mechanisms involving computer systems and cyber conduct. Kenya Law records a 2025 amendment in the current version history.

## Boundary with data protection

A privacy or data-protection concern is **not automatically a cybercrime**. Criminal offences have their own legal elements, evidence and procedural safeguards. KDR should therefore surface potentially relevant provisions for education while avoiding labels such as “hacking”, “cybercrime” or “illegal access” unless evidence supports them.

## Useful evidence distinctions

- content data versus traffic/subscriber information;
- authorized versus unauthorized access;
- data disclosure versus system interference;
- harassment/unwanted contact versus a technical system offence.

Authoritative source: Kenya Law, Computer Misuse and Cybercrimes Act (Cap. 79C).
