# Chapter 6 — Access to information, consumer and communications law

KDR's core is data protection, but related questions can engage other frameworks.

- **Access to Information Act:** access to information held by public bodies and defined private-body circumstances where information is needed to exercise/protect rights.
- **Consumer Protection Act:** unfair practices, representations, agreements and remedies.
- **Kenya Information and Communications Act:** communications-sector/electronic-transaction framework. Verify commencement status and later regulations before relying on a particular provision.

These frameworks should be cross-referenced rather than merged into a single “privacy law” score.
