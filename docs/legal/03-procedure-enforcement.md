# Chapter 3 — ODPC registration, complaints and enforcement

The Registration Regulations describe when and how controllers/processors register and how the ODPC register is maintained. A processor that goes beyond a controller's instructions can become a controller for that processing activity.

The Complaints Handling and Enforcement Regulations provide a procedure for lodging complaints and for ODPC enforcement/penalty processes.

## KDR evidence language

- Say **“not located in the reviewed public snapshot”**, not “unregistered”.
- Keep registration status separate from a substantive privacy allegation.
- Keep ODPC determinations separate from later court outcomes.
- Preserve the exact source snapshot and review date.

## Complaint preparation

KDR may help a user organize facts, dates, screenshots, sender identifiers and rights requests, but should not fabricate missing evidence or promise an ODPC outcome.

Authoritative sources: Kenya Law Legal Notices 264 and 265 of 2021; ODPC official publications.
